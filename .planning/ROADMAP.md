# Roadmap: MOX-ADV P0 Completion

## Overview

This milestone finishes only the current P0 module through the four owner-defined business submodules. It builds a neural-agent-led analytics contour, formalizes only material owner decisions, develops evidence-backed marketing strategies, and realizes them as an editable canvas of viable current-format Yandex campaigns. Deterministic acceptance requires at least one `VIABLE` Draft; one separately authorized official-API acceptance then proves non-serving creation. External landing development, post-launch optimization, Dashboard integration, and later modules remain outside this roadmap.

## Phases

- [ ] **Phase 1: Data Collection and Analytics** - Add the trusted neural agent and complete company, focus, market, Direct, Metrika, auction, and destination evidence.
- [ ] **Phase 2: Questionnaire and Formalization** - Lead with an editable recommended goal and ask only for material unresolved business choices.
- [ ] **Phase 3: Marketing Strategy Development** - Convert accepted evidence and focus into complete explainable Strategy alternatives.
- [ ] **Phase 4: Marketing Strategy Realization** - Build the editable campaign canvas, current Direct projections, viability, shortlist, and safe package execution.
- [ ] **Phase 5: Deterministic P0 Acceptance** - Prove agent behavior, safety, business UI, and at least one viable campaign without real writes.
- [ ] **Phase 6: Authorized Live P0 Acceptance** - Validate one exact official-API package while every campaign remains non-serving.

## Phase Details

### Phase 1: Data Collection and Analytics

**Goal**: Let one bounded neural agent autonomously collect enough trustworthy current evidence to recommend what the company should advertise and identify the exact destination/readiness constraints.
**Depends on**: Nothing (first phase)
**Requirements**: AGT-01, AGT-02, AGT-03, AGT-04, AGT-05, AGT-06, FOC-01, FOC-02, EVD-01, EVD-02, EVD-03, EVD-04, EVD-05, MET-01, LND-01, LND-02, LND-03
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. A real model iterates through bounded typed tools while the P0 application alone enforces objectives, authority, schemas, budgets, persistence, side effects, and final truth.
  2. The agent inventories materially distinct products/services and, when several exist, recommends a focus through comparable opportunity cards that the owner can later edit.
  3. Competitor observations, full Direct configuration/reports, Wordstat demand, qualifying cost, auction hypotheses, and Metrika readiness are complete for scope or honestly unavailable; competitor success is never invented.
  4. The exact destination is classified as existing site page, existing landing, missing future landing, or invalid target; existing pages receive measured analysis plus explicitly labelled hypotheses and at most three corrections.
  5. Runs resume from durable checkpoints, treat public content as untrusted, and apply only an active versioned official-source playbook.

### Phase 2: Questionnaire and Formalization

**Goal**: Present the agent’s recommended business formalization first and involve the owner only where a material business choice remains.
**Depends on**: Phase 1
**Requirements**: UX-01, UX-02, UX-03, UX-04, UX-05, AUTH-01, AUTH-02, AUTH-03, AUTH-04
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. The campaign goal appears among the first owner-visible fields, is prefilled with an evidence-backed recommendation, remains editable, and explains any later recommended revision.
  2. The owner sees findings, recommendation, consequence, decision, next action, and outcome without IDs, hashes, schemas, API methods, payloads, journals, or raw diagnostics.
  3. Discoverable facts are filled automatically; the module asks one prepared question at a time only for authority, irreversibility, Mandate excess, or Material Uncertainty.
  4. Owner edits create a complete revisioned canonical Strategy and invalidate dependent artifacts without requiring the owner to understand implementation terminology.

### Phase 3: Marketing Strategy Development

**Goal**: Turn accepted focus and analytics into complete, evidence-backed advertising strategies and explicit hypotheses.
**Depends on**: Phase 2
**Requirements**: STR-01, STR-02, STR-03, STR-04
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. The agent produces a complete Strategy covering focus, outcome, offer, audience, demand clusters, positioning, placements, budget/economics, attribution, geography, schedule, seasonality, measurement, and destination.
  2. Every competitor, demand, auction, targeting, creative, and placement hypothesis states evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, expected mechanism, affected Drafts, and a verification path.
  3. Sparse conversion or cost evidence produces an explicit fallback strategy rather than fabricated certainty or an unsuitable conversion objective.
  4. Fan-out input contains finite approved product/offer/audience/demand axes and does not use post-launch outcomes or claim an effective winner before serving.

### Phase 4: Marketing Strategy Realization

**Goal**: Realize each approved Strategy as a finite editable canvas of defensible campaign hypotheses and an exact safe Direct package.
**Depends on**: Phase 3
**Requirements**: VIA-01, VIA-02, VIA-03, DIR-01, DIR-02, DIR-03, DIR-04, EXEC-01, EXEC-02
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. The campaign canvas shows materially distinct editable control/improvement Drafts, every hidden/blocked disposition, and a manual shortlist without losing internal audit lineage.
  2. Hard eligibility runs before scoring; comparable eligible Drafts show evidence coverage, sensitivity, score/rank, and `VIABLE | TESTABLE_WITH_GAPS | INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE | BLOCKED` without prediction language.
  3. The exact account capability matrix selects only applicable current features, including combinatorial `RESPONSIVE_AD` and eligible targeting/assets/tracking, and blocks every unsupported field rather than silently dropping it.
  4. One exact owner-authorized package persists intent, creates each item independently, suspends and semantically reads it back, continues moderation durably, never retries an ambiguous write, and exposes no resume/serving capability.

### Phase 5: Deterministic P0 Acceptance

### Phase 6: Authorized Live P0 Acceptance

**Goal**: Demonstrate that the accepted P0 creates and moderates an exact package through official APIs while remaining non-serving.
**Depends on**: Phase 5
