MOX-ADV is AI-first. Responsibility is divided by authority and consequence rather than by whether an action is internal or external.

The default operating posture is autonomous completion of safe work: the agent discovers facts, prepares business artifacts, performs routine bounded actions inside an active Mandate, and retains evidence. The exception is a Human Decision Gate for a Critical Decision or Material Uncertainty.

This context aligns with the repository's current ubiquitous language in `CONTEXT.md`:

- **Agent-Owned Work** is work the agent is expected to complete without unnecessary operator handoffs.
- **Critical Decision** changes authority or creates materially irreversible consequences.
- **Material Uncertainty** can materially alter the business outcome.
- **Human Decision Gate** pauses only for one prepared human decision with options, evidence, confidence, consequences, and a recommendation.
