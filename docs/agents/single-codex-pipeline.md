# Running the pipeline with one Codex

Read this document before starting or continuing MOX-ADV campaign preparation. It applies to every Codex session, not one conversation.

## Ownership

The human owns and confirms Goal. You are the only reasoning agent for the remaining three stages: Сбор сведений → Стратегия → Кампании. Do not change the human's goal, spawn stage agents or subagents, use an internal model adapter, start a subscription bridge for the pipeline, or delegate a stage to another model. The same Codex investigates, evaluates evidence, chooses the next action, writes the strategy and campaigns, and fixes rejected proposals.

Scripts are tools, not decision makers. Use them for permitted source reads, extraction, arithmetic, schema checks, references, compilation and persistence. Keep source facts separate from hypotheses and uncertainties. Existing field limits are ceilings, not content quotas. Missing results remain unavailable, and a hypothesis never becomes evidence for an advertising promise.

## Current goal and preparation contract (v4)

New starts freeze `goal-directed-preparation-v4`. Existing Goal revisions and preparation versions remain sealed and readable; do not silently relabel an old result as reviewed by v4. The owner confirms Goal in the UI. Counts, paid orders, sums in RUB (revenue/profit) and proportions retain distinct event definitions, units, cancellation/refund rules and measurement definitions. Proportions require a denominator definition and a positive minimum volume. Unsupported units or formulas must be clarified rather than executed or converted to lead counts. Legacy qualified-request goals use their already confirmed counting policy. Editing the result type clears the draft outcome and target for explicit owner entry; Codex must not change the current human Goal to exercise another type.

Research outputs `research.goal_requirements`, bound to the exact Goal revision and digest. Cover audience, offer, reach, landing/action, measurement, budget, time and capacity; add payment, reversals, cost composition or denominator where the goal requires them. Distinguish supported facts, assumptions, unavailability, conflict and known blockers. Each unresolved material requirement has a decision gap with performed checks, consequence and next action. A stated goal proves neither demand nor a working payment/measurement system. Only budget and timing may use `OWNER_GOAL` as support. Record outcome evidence with the actual event, unit, population, dates, relation to Goal, evidence class and permitted use. Form events, clicks, unrelated products and ordinary ad rotation cannot be relabelled as qualified outcomes, paid orders or causal evidence.

Strategy outputs `goal_preparation.outcome_plan`: make a decision for each requirement and compare every considered approach as a complete package (audience, offer, landing, target action, measurement, budget and timing). State the strongest opposing case. `UNRESOLVED` ranking is allowed with a practical provisional selection and an explanation of the unknown that could change it. Do not fabricate a winner, probability or numeric coefficients merely to finish. A supported numerical scenario is conditional on its sources and assumptions; it is not empirical validation of the preparation mechanism.

`COUNT` uses the exact counted-event funnel and explicit duplicate/deadline adjustments. `SUM` and `RATIO` use `forecast.metric_inputs` for every direction, with `inputs: []` and both funnel adjustments `null`: these inputs already incorporate the owner rules. Money is in RUB, profit may be negative and includes advertising expenses once. Proportions sum numerator and denominator counts from comparable nonoverlapping cohorts; never average percentages. Inputs include their exact budget, population and adjustments. Changing a budget invalidates a generic projection instead of scaling it. Record `OUTCOME_VALUE` or `DENOMINATOR` when unavailable. An incremental-effect Goal needs linked causal evidence, not just an attributed conversion report.

Campaigns outputs `goal_review.outcome_review`, referencing every requirement and every actual selected/rejected candidate. Its `creative_combinations` covers each actual title/body combination with the exact strings and a compatibility judgment; any material change invalidates that coverage, and a conflict requires repair. Link resolved preparation defects to real `optimization_review.issues` repairs and actual affected objects. External or unmeasured performance conditions remain conditions. `success_probability` must be `null`: a calibrated probabilistic model is not implemented. The existing draft → critique → revision workflow still applies. Explicit local completion uses `preparation_decision` / `READY_FOR_VALIDATION`; v4 observation fields include `GOAL_OUTCOME_RECORDS` and `GOAL_OUTCOME_DATE`, plus amounts/corrections or numerator/denominator when applicable. Missing observations do not become zero or a completed measurement.

The owner UI shows independent preparation, goal-support and technical-check statuses. Technical checks cover the prepared local material only. They do not certify provider acceptance, moderation or business results. The dated Direct profile now enforces at most three new nonarchived `RESPONSIVE_AD` objects per unified group. Compatible executions can share one object, but every allowed title/body combination must preserve the offer and all material conditions. Review actual PNG/JPEG/GIF assets and leave account creation, publication and spending to their existing authority boundaries.

Do all routine work yourself. The owner does not need to prepare JSON, operate the tools, approve each stage, or repeat information already saved. Ask only about a material business ambiguity or authority outside the approved task.

## Offline tools

### Search coverage and completion

Before preparing Search campaigns, define the decisions that research can change: qualified buyer intent, funded groups, differentiated copy, meaningful budget allocation or material wasted traffic. Prioritize queries by that decision impact. Review the useful existing observations, pending candidates, exclusions and collection failures. An existing measured subset is a starting point; `REAL_INPUTS` does not authorize inventing frequencies. It also does not require measuring every variant or collecting irrelevant tails.

Use “Дополнительные запросы Wordstat” in the agent tools to collect explicit queries through the configured source collector. Import a verified source snapshot first. Each phase accepts up to 20 queries in the goal's region; this is an operational batch limit, not a limit on total research. Read popular and similar queries where they can expose new intent, then verify the resulting candidates and necessary operator forms. Each phase appends an immutable dated source material. Existing observations and source gaps remain intact until the agent evaluates the combined evidence.

Before completing Campaigns, retain a review that establishes all of the following:

- Every material intent area has been investigated, included or explicitly excluded for a business reason. State what a further source result could change before collecting it. Leave low-impact variants uncollected with a concise scope reason; do not treat that as an unresolved blocker.
- Review the useful expansion results for qualification and distinct intent. Assign selected phrases to groups and record material exclusions. Avoid collecting or copying long irrelevant tails, exhaustive word-form variants and overlapping keys merely to increase counts.
- Every group has a review of its effective campaign and group negatives, concrete unwanted intents, overlaps with positive keys and examples of qualified queries that must remain eligible. A copied general negative list is not sufficient evidence of this review.
- The reason for stopping describes why further research is unlikely to change the important campaign decisions under the owner's constraints. Compiler acceptance, successful saving, elapsed time and a chosen phrase count are not completion criteria. If a material unresolved gap could change funded scope, investigate it; otherwise retain the limitation and complete the useful preparation.

Summarize the review and material limits briefly in the owner UI. Its details contain business comparisons and readable public-source citations. Keep the technical candidate ledger, reference IDs and collection diagnostics in the dedicated agent work area. Technical completion, assessed search coverage, publishability and evidence of commercial results remain separate states.

After a result is submitted, inspect its rendered stage, saved content and limitations. A successful compiler run alone does not establish end-to-end success. A final report distinguishes the checks actually run from live provider operations that were not performed.

### Perform available prelaunch checks before declaring a gap

An available read-only check is the controlling Codex's work, not an owner checklist item. Inspect the rendered landing and its actual forms: relevant fields, platform validation (not only HTML `required`), submission integration, configured success destination and the destination's real content, and linked offer documents for the correct edition and conditions. Inspect the tracking counter actually loaded on the landing, then check that exact counter and its goals through the permitted API; an unrelated configured account is not a substitute. Record source, time, action, observed result and consequence. A missing check is unfinished work; a performed request returning access denied is a concrete access gap.

Keep three distinctions explicit: defects that can be found before launch; checks blocked by a named missing integration/access or an external action outside the authorization; and outcomes observable only after real traffic and business handling. A public offer does not disclose live inventory, staff throughput or CRM receipt. A client-side dry run, intercepted request, success callback or three configured form-service bindings does not prove that a real message reached its recipient. Do not send test enquiries to third parties without explicit authorization or invent successful delivery. Ordinary future conversion uncertainty must not be phrased as a routine task the owner should have completed before preparation.

## Evidence-to-campaign method

New runs use `evidence-to-campaign-v1`, generalized from the INNOPROM HTML preparation method. The reference is the process, not its fixed four campaigns, budgets or keyword count.

- Research covers offer, buyers, objections, formats/calendar, competitors, contractor cases, demand, landing, measurement and economics. Assign a stable ID to every material finding. Preserve observations, inferred conclusions, missing data, conflicts and empty query results separately. The declared coverage must account for all findings.
- Strategy retains the exact human Goal and produces a `formation_plan`. Every finding receives one disposition: APPLIED, DEFERRED or EXCLUDED, with reason and target. Directions specify channels, intent, audience, offer and weekly caps. Plan budget phases, experiment variants, landing qualification and result measurement. Seek additional permitted evidence when it changes a decision; import a new complete snapshot through the UI before relying on it.
- Campaigns implement every direction and variant, with linked groups, ads, images, search keywords or network themes/segments, exclusions and phase allocations. Every APPLIED finding maps to real object IDs. Limits are resource ceilings, not quotas. Fixed variants must be separate ads, and segment comparisons must not be presented as controlled A/B results.
- A new local graph stores the complete intended plan, not a partial Search projection. The provider mapping and publication remain unavailable until separately implemented and checked. No local completion authorizes spending.

When the human authorizes test data, select “Тестовый прогон” before starting. Record every substitution in `research.test_data`: value, what could not be found, attempted sources and affected decisions. Use such values only as explicitly linked TEST_DATA planning inputs; they cannot certify source facts, advertising claims, live audience readiness or actual campaign metrics. REAL_INPUTS runs reject test substitutions. The Dashboard shows this table in Research, Strategy and Campaigns so the human can see exactly what the agent did not establish.

The optional typed metric and direction on a planning input feed scenario arithmetic (CPC, CTR, form conversion, qualification, close rate, contribution margin). Missing numbers propagate as unknown. A retargeting toggle shows the conditional reserve separately; the actuals column remains empty.

Previously collected external research may be included as `business_research.supporting_materials` in a new source snapshot. Preserve its original date, primary source URLs and whether the material is a research summary or official observations. Such summaries are context for reasoning, not first-party copy proof. The verified snapshot and task digest cover the complete material. Source collection, analysis acceptance, local plan completeness and publishability remain different states.

## Choose campaigns for the owner's outcome

The purpose of preparation is to produce the campaigns and ads that, in your considered judgment, offer the strongest supported route to the exact human Goal. You own this judgment. Do not finish merely because research was recorded, an ad is topically relevant, or a compiler accepted a graph. Do not offload the choice to scripts, stage agents or another model.

The v3 foundation below continues to apply to count goals in v4, with the metric-specific additions above. Strategy requires `formation_plan.goal_preparation`; Campaigns requires `goal_review`. Historical v1/v2/v3 workspaces remain readable and are not retrospectively certified by v4. To apply the current method to a previously completed result, continue with verified saved evidence in a new preparation run through the UI.

1. Research the buying decision: explicit and mixed intent, reasons to choose this offer, objections, proof, reachable demand and seasonality, competing approaches, landing qualification and comparable historical outcomes. Resolve a material research gap before choosing a weaker campaign just to fill the output schema.
2. Compare genuinely competing approaches. Select the strongest supported one and explain why it is preferable to the alternatives under the actual goal and budget. Keep a concise decision record, not private reasoning traces. A rejected approach may combine channels or represent a materially different offer or audience; the comparison is not a quota for campaigns to produce.
3. For every funded direction, justify commercial intent, the route to a qualified result and its budget relative to the alternatives. Identify exploration explicitly. Preserve the exact human outcome, minimum target count, deadline, region and total budget. The fixed objective is to achieve the target with minimum spending within that ceiling; leave the remainder unallocated. Do not ask for an optimization checkbox or a separate target result price. Total budget divided by target count is an internal planning ceiling, not observed CPA. A plan for one month must explain its contribution to a longer goal; it cannot silently claim to cover the whole goal or assume further funding.
4. Assess feasibility using evidence-supported ranges for CPC, click-to-qualified-result conversion and obtainable clicks in the planned period. Every numeric range has source references and a derivation or transfer explanation. Mark estimates `INFERENCE`; missing inputs remain `UNKNOWN` and null. An estimate is neither observed fact nor authorized test substitution. Never derive unique clicks from summed Wordstat frequencies, transfer an unrelated conversion goal, or select optimistic numbers backwards from the owner's target. The deterministic calculation limits clicks by demand and reports scenario bounds; it does not estimate a probability of success.
5. For each group, develop competing, grounded ads and choose the most persuasive response to that buyer's intent and objections. Record the selected texts and at least one substantive rejected alternative with reasons. Selected candidates must exactly match the actual ads and their factual sources. Prefer concrete verified benefits and a qualified next action. Adapt repeated generic copy, or explain why reuse is the better choice for both groups. Keep field limits as ceilings, not quotas.
6. Challenge the complete portfolio against demand and intent, offer and copy, landing and qualification, budget and goal, and evidence limits. Repair material weaknesses yourself and update the affected objects. If the defect is upstream, return to Strategy or research through the existing UI workflow. Do not relabel a blocker as a limitation to finish. `REWORK_REQUIRED`, `RESEARCH_REQUIRED` and any `BLOCKER` prevent acceptance of Campaigns. Ask the owner only when a material business uncertainty cannot be resolved from permitted sources.
7. `BEST_SUPPORTED` is your comparative recommendation, not a claim about the odds of reaching the numeric target. `SUPPORTED_BY_ESTIMATE` requires a full-goal conservative scenario within the budget. Missing performance remains `UNASSESSED` or `CONDITIONAL`. Local preparation can nevertheless finish when the full permitted prelaunch work is complete and `goal_review.preparation_decision` records `LOCAL_CAMPAIGN_PREPARATION` / `READY_FOR_VALIDATION`. This decision must account for every unavailable CPC, qualification, reachable-click, duplicate or delay input; bind an existing positive budget phase and the exact owner-qualified result; describe collection of cost, clicks, unique qualified results, qualification dates and campaign/ad attribution; and state remaining-goal reassessment, stop rules and prepublication dependencies. These conditions do not authorize a launch or certify effectiveness. Known adverse scenarios and material prelaunch blockers still require repair. Without an explicit complete decision, v3 preserves the working portfolio in the UI and `source.saved_campaign_candidate` and the agent continues. An explicitly authorized test remains TEST_ONLY. Never treat successful schema checks, local completion or a scenario as proof of commercial results.

The Dashboard exposes the recommendation, goal contribution, scenario limits and reasons for choosing ads. Inspect that saved result before reporting completion. This preparation contract does not add publication or spend authority.

Owner-facing findings, summaries, reasons and limitations use plain business language. Do not put reference IDs, hashes, field names, schema/version labels, diagnostic messages, collection passes, query-operator mechanics or file paths in that prose. Keep those details in internal task data and dedicated reference fields, available through `/agent`. Explain the business consequence instead: which information is unavailable, which choice follows, and which numerical claim remains unconfirmed. Preserve public sources, observation dates, geography, actual prices, uncertainty and literal advertising text. This is a presentation boundary; it does not change evidence requirements or acceptance rules.

### Portfolio search, minimum spending and creation examples

Continue investigating after the first acceptable campaign. Cover buyers, intents, offer angles, channels, geography/calendar and first-party history; judge further collection by whether it can improve the actual portfolio. `portfolio_search` accounts for every material candidate, its additional value and selection/disposition. Every selected candidate becomes a real direction and campaign. One campaign needs a specific reason after the broader search; multiple copies of the same approach do not satisfy this obligation. Missing historical conversions do not impose a one-template cap.

There is no total campaign, group or ad count ceiling. Produce all viable additions that improve the supported prospect of attaining the exact goal or reduce spending without weakening that prospect. Quantity is not the objective: one and a thousand are equally acceptable. For v3 each candidate has a sourced `viability` assessment covering its contribution, budget/deadline fit and overlaps with other candidates. Every viable positive contribution must be selected; redundant, harmful or ungrounded options receive an explicit disposition. Each actual ad records its additional value and overlap with the compared creatives. Changing names, adding synonyms, or fragmenting the same demand does not create value. Inspect quality across the complete portfolio; stop at diminishing decision value, never at an object count or exhausted output schema.

The fixed priority is the best supported chance of satisfying all Goal constraints together, with the least supported spending among comparably credible portfolios. A cheaper but materially less credible route to the goal is not a better solution. Do not assign numerical success probabilities without a defensible statistical model and applicable data.

`analyze-plan` returns missing inputs, a minimum spending bound and a rounded candidate allocation under the supplied ranges, available result capacity, and any budget or capacity shortfall. It does not change the selected portfolio. Inspect independent reachable demand, constant CPC/conversion, overlap and timing assumptions before using that candidate. Its bound applies to the specified model; it is not a proven market minimum. It returns diagnostics even when the proposed allocation needs correction. Absence remains unknown and cannot be solved by collecting more irrelevant keyword variants.

Record additional research questions and decision-relevant metrics in `investigations`. Resolve material open questions through permitted sources. Actual source unavailability stays explicit. Do not ask the owner to assemble routine evidence, operate JSON tools or choose campaign counts. Stop when further investigation is unlikely to change an important decision, not when one campaign or a fixed number of keywords exists.

For v2/v3 forecasts, account for duplicate results across campaigns and the share that can be qualified before the deadline. Unknown adjustments keep the aggregate estimate unknown. Compare the selected budget allocation with a cheaper option under the same assumptions. If a cheaper considered allocation supports the full target with comparable or stronger support, select it or correct the assumptions that made the comparison misleading. In v3, use `goal_support_vs_selected` for that comparison. A more expensive plan is allowed when the cheaper alternative has WEAKER support and its rationale explains the evidence and risk difference. Scenario expected counts alone cannot establish equal odds of reaching the target; an unassessed comparison requires further work. The complete preparation budget must remain within the owner's total, across every campaign and phase.

The owner now enters `total_budget_rub` in Goal v3. Historical `max_result_cost_rub` remains a per-result limit in its original sealed revision. Never reinterpret or multiply an old amount into an owner-confirmed total. New starts require the owner to provide the total budget in the first stage; already active historical workspaces remain resumable.

### Cold start without prior performance

Absence of comparable history does not cap the number of useful templates. Finish the permitted research of buyers, offer, objections, commercial intent, landing, alternatives and overlap. Retain every viable addition and a bounded measurement phase inside the same total budget, identifying what qualified observations will revise the selection and remaining-goal assessment. Do not substitute form events for unique qualified outcomes or manufacture transferable conversion ranges. If the owner says the history does not exist, record that in the saved review and do not repeatedly request it. After completing all material prelaunch work, use the explicit preparation decision to finish local preparation with unknown numeric attainment and a bounded measurement plan. Do not leave the owner in a repeated research loop for unavailable history. Unfinished work remains resumable. A plan to gather performance evidence cannot itself certify the goal, and it grants no authority to set up an external system, publish or spend.

### Durable stage instructions and autonomy

Each exported task contains versioned stage instructions, the frozen human Goal, the complete relevant inputs, the expected output schema and allowed source references. Read and apply these on every stage; content judgment adapts to the business, but the method is not improvised from memory. Evidence identifies actionable facts and gaps; Strategy accounts for every finding and selects complementary viable directions under one budget; Campaigns implements and challenges all directions and creatives. After successful saving, obtain the next task and continue without asking the owner to steer routine steps. Return upstream when new evidence changes a decision. Completion requires an explicit review of the preparation scope, separate from numerical feasibility, publication readiness and actual goal attainment. New repeatable behavior belongs in these instructions and the task contract with regression checks.

New starts additionally freeze `campaign_optimization_version: goal-campaign-optimization-v1`. This is a responsibility of the pipeline agent on every run, including future sessions: after the owner defines Goal and starts preparation, investigate, compare, select, challenge and repair until the strongest supported local ads and settings are saved. A one-off chat recommendation does not implement that responsibility. The wake message and each stage's `context.campaign_optimization` carry this objective. Existing sealed runs keep their original contract and are not silently labelled as newly reviewed.

Campaigns in these new runs require `optimization_review` for every actual group. Review six parameter families: audience/intent and exclusions; offer/copy; images and extensions; landing and qualification; delivery; and the shared budget and timing. For each, compare concrete alternatives or explain why the setting is held. Holding a useful headline or strategy constraint is valid. Offer/copy must include a real rejected text already recorded in `goal_review`. State the alternative's strongest advantage, principal risk and the reason for selecting the actual value. Include the strongest counterargument to each group's overall selection and the business reason for stopping further search. There is no prescribed number of output campaigns or forced A/B test. All these are prelaunch judgments; ordinary ad variants are not measured or randomized winners.

Use the offline inspection command to obtain exact final parameter values without generating any judgment:

node scripts/pipeline-tools.mjs optimization-inputs /absolute/task.json /absolute/candidate.json /absolute/optimization-inputs.json

Copy these values into the corresponding `selected_value_json` fields only after making the substantive decision. An alternative's `value_json` contains the same parameter object with concrete changed values. These JSON strings are internal agent data and must not be shown as owner-facing explanations. The compiler checks that selected values still match the final ads, audiences, assets, landing, delivery and budgets. If any changes, revisit the affected comparison; merely refreshing a serialized value is not an adequate review. An alternative requiring a different accepted strategy must be resolved upstream, then reviewed against fresh task materials.

Record discovered preparation defects and their actual before/after parameter values. `OPEN` defects and defects relabelled as `ACCEPTED_LIMITATION` block acceptance. `REPAIRED` must correspond to a concrete change in a related final object. `PERFORMANCE_UNKNOWN` can remain an accepted limitation; editing copy cannot repair unknown commercial results. The existing explicit cold-start completion decision still applies. Deterministic checks establish linkage and completeness, not the truth of the agent's persuasive assessment.

When the server rejects a submitted candidate, its proposal and validation feedback are saved in the durable workspace. The next task includes `repair_context`, even after releasing the lease and continuing in another session. Inspect and fix that candidate, or return upstream if the feedback exposes a research or strategy defect. The next task has a fresh digest; old envelopes cannot be reused. Successful acceptance or updated research clears obsolete repair feedback. The owner should not troubleshoot JSON or repeat stage instructions.

### Mandatory saved-draft critique and refinement

This is a sequence of tasks for the same controlling Codex, not another agent, provider call or claim of an independent evaluation. The existing UI upload and task-download actions are sufficient; the owner does not operate additional steps.

1. **DRAFT:** create actual campaigns and ads from the exact Goal, accepted evidence and strategy. Use existing campaigns only as references. Compare the best credible alternatives; do not make a deliberately vague text the only challenger to a preferred ad. Identify buyer intent, useful verified benefits or conditions, commercial qualification, objections and landing continuity. Keep the existing goal and optimization reviews in the candidate. Do not supply `refinement_review` yourself. A valid submission persists a provisional draft, leaves Campaigns active, and returns a new task.
2. **REVIEW:** read the frozen `source.campaign_refinement.draft` and its digest. The output schema is now a critique, not another portfolio. Review every actual ad for buyer fit, value/proof, qualified action, landing continuity, clarity and factual scope. Quote exact visible ad fragments and cite real finding IDs. Compare each ad with a different real candidate for its group. An unmeasured benefit hypothesis stays a hypothesis. Dates, costs and guarantees must not be invented. Do not mark a generic CTA as persuasive merely because it is valid, or a dense source quotation as clear merely because it is true.
3. **REVISE:** for each material weakness, record its issue ID, affected ads, repair scope, problem and required change. Set `WEAK` and `REVISE`/`REMOVE` consistently; return `REVISE`. The next task preserves the draft, critique and prior reviews across sessions. Change the criticized content/settings or remove the weak ad. Updating IDs, punctuation, names or justifications cannot discharge the issue. Get the next task and review the revised draft again. There is no arbitrary number of refinement rounds.
4. If a correction needs research or a strategy change, set `research_required` and use the existing “Дополнительное исследование” action. The backend prevents a local rewrite from clearing that requirement. Evidence receives `required_refinement_findings`; answer every issue with that exact finding ID and include it in its coverage area. Observed/inferred answers require their source support; unavailable or conflicting answers retain their decision consequence. The prior draft and questions remain in task data through research and strategy; simply returning upstream does not resolve them, and the old critique cannot approve a newly grounded portfolio.
5. **ACCEPT:** finish the critique only when every ad is covered, no material weakness remains, and the best feasible alternatives have been considered. Explain why further preparation is unlikely to change a material choice. The server recompiles its saved draft, attaches the final critique and previous critiques, and applies the existing full-Goal/cold-start completion checks. A missing explicit completion decision or a known full-goal shortfall still keeps work active. The sealed review is bound to the actual portfolio digest and is rechecked during compilation of later edits.

The offline `validate`/`package` commands use the same workflow evaluator as the server. `CONTINUE_REFINEMENT` means the next task is REVIEW or REVISION; `VERIFIED_PRODUCT` is subject to server persistence and Goal readiness. Inspect actual UI stage state after saving. A state-machine transition is not a quality judgment: you own the critique and must substantively improve weaknesses rather than refresh its bookkeeping.

Copy grounding allows ordinary evidence-grounded wording beside a complete protected source unit. A dated statement or conditional price must remain intact with all its conditions; other supported wording may be adapted for clarity. Do not solve rejected copy by dropping conditions or adding new claims. For example, a complete dated event statement may accompany a shorter grounded category name. Research and attach a proper source when a useful claim is missing; the compiler cannot create its truth.

A standalone named edition in a neutral source heading can also be used exactly, such as `ИННОПРОМ-2027` from `Условия участия в ИННОПРОМ-2027`. The name and year must remain unchanged. This extraction does not apply to a sentence containing eligibility, cancellation, price or deadline conditions. A category headline may carry its full minimum/step condition in the body only when every responsive combination preserves the same subject and all conditions. Reordering or repeating existing title/body variants cannot resolve a copy defect.

### Method ownership and transfer preparation

The owner judges useful ads and progress toward the exact Goal, not cosmetic variation or a particular preparation technique. The controlling Codex owns campaign counts, repeated versus different headlines, creative variants and experiment selection. A repeated headline is permitted when it has buyer value or intentionally holds a comparison constant. A/B testing is an instrument for a material decision, not a mandatory extra campaign or proof that ordinary ad rotation is randomized.

Before accepting new Campaigns, check the current Direct creation contract and supported asset format. The present content profile corresponds to ResponsiveAd in unified groups; template-only ImageRefs, VariantRef, ValidUntil, phase allocations and semantic metadata are not literal fields for Ads.add. Subsequent transfer must resolve uploaded image hashes, group IDs and extension IDs and implement scheduling and spend control. Do not claim provider acceptance without a provider result.

New candidates are checked against the dated direct_preparation_rules supplied in the task. Max-clicks weekly ruble budgets must be at least 300 RUB; changes must preserve the owner's total and the strategy's shared allocation. Prepared image assets need PNG/JPEG/GIF files, valid standard or 16:9 dimensions, byte size and a SHA-256. SVG may remain an editable master. Inspect the actual exported image, not only its filename. Package verifies local asset bytes and metadata; public remote assets require an observed inspection before their metadata is entered. Refresh these version-sensitive rules from official documentation when changing the provider profile. These are bounded technical preparation checks, not full API creation, moderation, campaign-level publication readiness or effectiveness certification. Historical sealed products remain readable.

Use `node scripts/pipeline-tools.mjs inspect-image LOCAL_FILE OUTPUT.json` to obtain actual asset metadata. `validate` and `package` compare local campaign images with these bytes and reject changed, missing or merely renamed files. This creates task-local metadata only, not Dashboard or provider state.

### Versioned keyword preparation

New starts freeze `keyword_preparation_version: keyword-preparation-v1`. Existing runs retain their saved contract. `research.keyword_research` records the advertiser's relation to the brand, seven expansion axes, meaningful intent families, a candidate pool, performed investigations and the stopping reason. Expand both within an intent and into missing commercial vocabulary. Exact source phrases, exact site language and authored combinations have separate origins; a source-grounded combination is still `CURATED`. No count quota or invented frequency is allowed. Search history and Wordstat must retain their actual dates, region, matching scope and limitations.

Strategy uses this map to choose funded intents, offer, landing and budget. Campaigns requires `keyword_review`: account for every candidate and link each INCLUDED row to an exact actual key and group. A COVERED candidate needs a specific selected key plus literal or explicitly justified morphological coverage. Semantic correspondence alone is an assumption and cannot certify that a material synonym is covered. A newly discovered useful candidate requires returning to research. Do not delete a meaningful family because its unmeasured variants are absent from the initial observed slice.

Review each search group's exact key list, campaign/group negatives, landing fit, incremental value and separate autotargeting scope. Include needed and unwanted query examples and explain every ignored full-overlap negative. Direct ignores a minus phrase when all its words overlap the positive key; it does not automatically block that key. The deterministic checker covers only conservative literal cases, and returns uncertainty for operators and nonliteral word forms. `AGENT_REVIEW` is a reasoned judgment, never a simulated provider result. New Search EPK preparation retains targeted autotargeting; keywords and autotargeting are assessed separately against actual query outcomes later.

The owner can inspect keys and group negatives beside the ads, campaign negatives inside the campaign, and readable selection reasons outside the technical archive. Internal IDs and serialized settings remain agent data. Finish after all material choices have been investigated and accounted for, not after a phrase count. Future authorized feedback uses spend, actual queries and the exact Goal outcome with qualification and dates; it does not equate clicks or form sends with qualified results.
