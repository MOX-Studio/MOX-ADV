# One Codex controls the complete campaign pipeline

Status: accepted by the owner on 2026-09-09.

The Production Dashboard keeps four stages: Goal, Evidence Collection, Strategy, Campaigns. The human owns and confirms Goal; one controlling Codex owns reasoning, research decisions, interpretation, strategy, campaign content, and repairs across the other three stages and sessions. The application does not instantiate stage agents, call model providers, or schedule another agent after a handoff.

This replaces the multi-agent implementation of ADR-0001. Its ownership principle remains: the controlling Codex completes routine permitted work and brings the owner only material decisions. Operators do not become responsible for writing stage JSON or manually doing research.

Deterministic tools collect specified sources, calculate values, prepare exact input contracts, validate supplied proposals, compile drafts, and persist verified revisions. A tool cannot invent a proposal, repair it by reasoning, choose an additional research objective, or call a model. Validation failure returns evidence and violations to the controlling Codex.

A durable workspace binds the run, exact input versions, source snapshot, interpretation, accepted strategy, controller lease, and pending commit. One session has the renewable 30-minute lease. A session can release it explicitly; after expiry a different session can claim it. Compare-and-swap revisions and task digests reject stale or concurrent results. Validation and commit are separate; the accepted pending result survives a service restart and can finish without replaying the accepted transition or duplicating audit events.

New runs record reasoning-stage authorship as `SINGLE_CODEX`. Historical artifacts, schemas and audit events remain readable; old agent modules remain only for legacy compatibility and regression tests, outside production composition. `POST /api/p0/agent` returns 410. Model/provider/bridge settings do not activate a stage agent.

Dashboard controls are the only interface to production pipeline state. Codex retrieves exact task materials through that UI, prepares a result with local tools, and submits it through the UI. Offline scripts never call Dashboard APIs or edit its database. Starting a run creates a workspace and queues a wake message to the bound existing Codex thread; it does not lease the workspace to the owner's browser. The controlling Codex claims the free lease and proceeds by explicit tool operations, without an internal model loop. The local adapter uses `codex queue`, with a fixed message and server-configured thread; it does not instantiate another agent. Waiting for that controller is displayed separately from actual execution.

Publication remains outside this preparation workflow. A completed local draft grants no publication or spend authority.

The owner's preparation objective also requires an explicit comparative judgment: the controlling Codex selects campaigns and ads for their expected contribution to the exact business goal. New workspaces freeze `goal-directed-preparation-v2`; strategy alternatives, goal-bound scenario inputs, selected and rejected creatives and a final critical review are persisted with the normal products. V1 products remain readable. V2 adds search beyond the first campaign, a total owner budget and comparison against lower spending, unique results received before the deadline, and grounded template extensions. Deterministic checks enforce references, budgets, arithmetic, selected-text identity and declared unresolved blockers. They do not assess persuasiveness, prove source assertions or assign a success probability. That judgment belongs to the controlling Codex. Historical products are not automatically assigned this assessment.

Owner clarification on 2026-09-09: there is no total campaign or ad count limit. V3 preparation requires candidate viability and marginal value, removes arbitrary collection count caps, and supplies deterministic scenario allocation diagnostics. The controlling Codex follows durable stage instructions from Goal through all three preparation stages without routine owner steering. Unknown performance in a cold start does not make finished prelaunch work incomplete: a reviewed `preparation_decision` can complete local preparation with an existing bounded validation phase, exact qualified-result measurement, preserved unknowns and stop rules. Numeric attainment remains unassessed and actual business outcome never becomes complete from preparation alone. Without that explicit decision, valid unfinished work remains durable and resumable; material blockers and known adverse scenarios still require repair. Historical products remain sealed. No scenario establishes statistical success probability or actual effectiveness, and local completion grants no publication or spending authority.
