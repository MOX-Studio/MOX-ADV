# MOX Studio bounded competitor matrix research

Date: 2026-08-24
Scope: Russian agencies with an exact public page for comprehensive branding, identity, or rebranding. This is a bounded comparison of published positioning, not a market ranking.

## Candidate-set rule

Four Russian agencies with a dedicated public page for comprehensive branding, identity, or rebranding were selected from a bounded public web search. Each candidate supplies a service comparable to at least one material part of MOX Studio’s confirmed offer: strategy, positioning, naming, identity, or brand guidelines.

## What this supports

- MOX Studio’s confirmed comprehensive branding/rebranding offer can be compared with exact public service positioning from four direct suppliers.
- All four observed pages describe strategy and/or identity work rather than only isolated production services.
- Published starting prices are page-local self-reported facts and may cover different scopes, taxes, and deliverables.

## What this does not support

- The matrix does not establish market share, advertising activity, spend, clicks, leads, conversion, commercial outcomes, or quality.
- The candidate set is not exhaustive and cannot establish prevalence.
- Search-ad visibility was not collected in this slice and must remain unavailable rather than zero.
- Prices are not normalized across scope, tax treatment, project complexity, or observation date.

## Strategy-aligned campaign hypotheses

The production matrix now treats each competitor row as a possible campaign-analysis unit. An observed advertisement can preserve its query, message, call to action, landing, geography, device, date, source, strategy fit, weakness, and one-family improvement hypothesis. When no advertisement was observed, the same analysis is explicitly labelled `HYPOTHESIS_FROM_PUBLIC_POSITIONING`; it must not be described as a launched campaign.

For the current four-page MOX slice, the recurring qualified pattern is: a comprehensive branding or rebranding offer on a dedicated service landing (`4/4`, 100% of this candidate set). The resulting market control preserves that broad offer. The treatment hypothesis narrows the audience to Russian B2B decision-makers, connects branding to business strategy, and optimizes the message toward a qualified project or cost-estimate request. This is a preregistered campaign hypothesis, not evidence of competitor performance.
